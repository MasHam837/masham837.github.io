What's in GILE?

  • Expanded End — Ghostly Thickets and Moon Desert.
  • Currencies — Primogems, Mora and elemental sigils.
  • Legendary weapons — Genshin-inspired weapons with custom abilities.
  • Creatures — Void Slimes, Moths, Weasels, Jerboas, Gloomy Lynx and Ghosts.
  • Abilities — Grappling Hook, Shrinker, Enlarger, Shadow Clock and Heart of Healing.
