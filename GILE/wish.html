Wishing Mechanic

The primary use of Primogems. A unique gacha-inspired system to obtain powerful Elemental and Legendary weapons through the Wish Table.

1. The Wish Table GUI

The entire mechanic revolves around the Wish Table, a functional block with a custom user interface. The UI consists of inputs, outputs, a central star, and a progression bar.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Wish Table GUI]
Show the interface with 2 input slots on the left, 2 output slots on the right, and the star/progress bar in the middle.
  • Input Slots (2): One strictly for Wish Blocks (the fuel), and one for an Elemental Sigil (the catalyst).
  • Output Slots (2): One for byproducts (Star Dust/Star Shine) and one for the successful result (Weapon).
Automation: The Wish Table can be automated! A hopper placed beneath the table will extract outputs. Note: The table will not start a new wish progress if the output slots are full.

2. The Wishing Process (Attempt)

Inputting one Elemental Sigil alongside Wish Blocks will begin the wishing process. Each attempt takes 5 seconds, consumes 1 Wish Block, and yields Star Dust, but does NOT consume the Elemental Sigil.

[GIF PLACEHOLDER: 5-Second Wish Mechanic Loop]
Show the star brightening in the GUI and colored particles emitting from the block in the world.

During an attempt, the central star brightens and emits colored particles (visible both in the GUI and in the world). The color determines how much the progression bar fills:

Rarity (Color)Chance per AttemptProgress Bar Fill %
Common (Blue)80%2% - 4%
Rare (Purple)17%15%
Legendary (Golden)3%25% - 50%

3. Wish Completion & Rewards

Once the progression bar fills to 100%, a weapon is generated in the output slot alongside a bonus of 10 Star Shine. The weapon you receive depends on a final roll based on the Elemental Sigil you used.

OutcomeChanceResult Details
Wish Success60%You receive the exact Elemental Weapon corresponding to your inputted sigil (e.g., Anemo Sigil gives an Anemo Weapon).
Legendary Success20%You receive 1 of the 4 Legendary Weapons.
Wish Fail20%You receive a random Elemental Weapon of a different element.
Pro Tip: Based on mathematical averages, a full stack of Wish Blocks (64) will typically yield around 4 weapons.

4. The Wisher Villager & Byproducts

The Wish Table also acts as a job site block, allowing an unemployed villager to take on the custom Wisher profession.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Wisher Villager Trades UI]
Showcase the custom trades available from the Wisher.

Currencies & Crafting

  • Star Shine: Obtained purely from Wish Completions. Used exclusively as high-tier currency to trade with the Wisher villager.
  • Star Dust: Obtained from every single Wish Attempt. Can be sold to the Wisher, or used to craft decorative Glitter Blocks and Glitter Carpets.
Note: Dust dropped by the Glitter mob found in the End Leftovers (see Biomes) can also be used interchangeably to craft Glitter Blocks and Carpets.

5. The Fate Weapon Set

During your wishing rolls, you may also occasionally obtain pieces of the Fate Weapon Set (Sword, Bow, and Arrow). Unlike the Elemental and Legendary weapons, the Fate set weapons are breakable (they have durability) and cannot be crafted.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Fate Sword, Bow, and Arrow items]
Showcase the stellar/galactic textures of the Fate set.

Unique On-Hit Effects

Though they break over time, they make up for it with powerful, fate-altering strikes:

  • Fate's Edge (Sword): On hit, has a chance to apply the Glowing effect to the target and temporarily steal their luck, boosting your chance of critical strikes for a few seconds.
  • Fate's String (Bow): Fires arrows with a flat trajectory that ignores wind and gravity physics.
  • Fate Arrow (Ammunition): When fired, this arrow rolls a "destiny check" on the target, applying a random severe debuff (Weakness, Slowness, or even briefly erasing their armor value).
