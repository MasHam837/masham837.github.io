What's in GILE?

  • Expanded Dimensions — Ghostly Thickets, Moon Desert & End Leftovers.
  • Entities — RuinGuard, Weasel Thiefs, Ghosts, Moth, Jerboa, Void Slime.
  • Decorative blocks — Pearlescent, Moon Sandstone, Ghostly Wood, Moon Fire and Glitter block sets.
  • Utilities — Grappling Hook, Shrinker, Enlarger, Warp Clock, Heart of Healing and Enderite equipment tool set.
  • Elemental Weapons — Genshin-inspired weapons with custom abilities for 8 elements.
  • Legendary Weapons — Even better rare imaginary weapons with more utility at higher cost.
