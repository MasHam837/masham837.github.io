Elemental Weapons

Unbreakable armaments of the Archons. Each weapon delivers constant damage and features a unique active ability.

Overview & Mechanics

Elemental weapons are exclusively obtained through the Wish Table. They are unbreakable and have no durability bar. Every elemental weapon has a unique ability bound to the V key (10-second cooldown), which typically triggers on the block you are looking at (except Hydro).

  • Bows: Unique draw mechanics with specialized projectiles.
  • Swords: Hit with AoE sweeps and apply slowness.
  • Spears: Slower attack speed, but damage scales with player speed.
  • Catalysts: Fast, precise, magic-based strikes.

Upgrading System

You was wishing on the same element and got too many same weapons? Don't worry, there's an upgrading system you can use. It'll make your ability stronger and your weapon much more powerful. You can upgrade your same elemental weapons just by combining them in a crafting grid. Normally, it's a duplicate logic. So merging two weapons of the same level gives you a weapon of the next level. (1+1=2, 2+2=3, 3+3=4, 4+4=5). Min is 1, max is 5. So you'll need exactly 16 weapons of level 1 to get one of level 5.

⚠ Data Pack Mode Notice: In Lunar Favor pack mode, weapon upgrading is additive. With it enabled, you just add levels of the weapons, which won't work normally. For example, one weapon on first level, and second on the third will give you a level four one. (1+2=3, 2+3=5, 1+4=5...). This makes it a lot easier to upgrade any weapon. You just need 5 weapons of the first level to get one on the fifth level.

You can turn it on by /datapack enable gilemod:lunar_favor_mode , you'll need cheets enabled on the server for that. OFF by default or by using /datapack disable gilemod:lunar_favor_mode
