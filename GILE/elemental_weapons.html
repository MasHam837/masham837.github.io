Elemental Weapons

Unbreakable armaments of the Archons. Each weapon delivers constant damage and features a unique active ability.

Overview & Mechanics

Elemental weapons are exclusively obtained through the Wish Table. They are unbreakable and have no durability bar. Every elemental weapon has a unique ability bound to the V key (10-second cooldown), which typically triggers on the block you are looking at (except Hydro).

  • Bows: Unique draw mechanics with specialized projectiles.
  • Swords: Hit with AoE sweeps and apply slowness.
  • Spears: Slower attack speed, but damage scales with player speed.
  • Catalysts: Fast, precise, magic-based strikes.

Anemo: Daybreak Chronicles (Bow)

Ability: Venti Vortex

[ITEM ICON]
Daybreak Chronicles
[GIF DEMO: Venti Vortex]
Showcase of the 8-block pulling vortex ability in action.
  • Passive: Grants the wielder complete immunity to fall damage.
  • Combat: Does not require arrows. Shoots unique "Venti Arrows". A small pull shoots normally. A hard pull creates a wind burst that pushes enemies. A max pull accelerates the arrow to create a massive wind burst capable of pushing even heavy mobs (Iron Golems, Wardens).
  • Active (V): Conjures a massive vortex with an 8-block radius that violently pulls in all mobs before pushing them away.

Geo: Vortex Vanquisher (Spear)

Ability: Zhong Li Barricade

[ITEM ICON]
Vortex Vanquisher
[GIF DEMO: Zhong Li Barricade]
Showcase of Geo columns rising from the ground and slowing enemies.
  • On-Hit: Generates Geo particles and slows enemies.
  • Active (V): Pulls Geo columns out of the ground to form a Barricade, heavily slowing every mob within range. After the 10s cooldown ends, the barricade retracts.
Surface Requirement: The ability only works if the surface contains enough Geo-aligned blocks.

Electro: Engulfing Lightning (Spear)

Ability: Raiden Shogun Thunder

[ITEM ICON]
Engulfing Lightning
[GIF DEMO: Raiden Shogun Thunder]
Showcase of thunderstorm activation and target lightning strike.
  • On-Hit: 50% chance to summon a lightning strike on the target.
  • Active (V): Summons a devastating thunderstorm. A huge bolt strikes the target block, while granting Resistance to the player and changing the world weather to Thunder. Once the cooldown ends, the weather clears.

Dendro: Thousand Floating Dreams (Catalyst)

Ability: Nahida Sowing

[ITEM ICON]
Thousand Floating Dreams
[GIF DEMO: Nahida Sowing]
Showcase of AoE poison field spreading on ground blocks.
  • Passive: Grants the player complete immunity to Poison and Wither effects.
  • On-Hit: Poisons the target for 5 seconds.
  • Active (V): Creates a massive Area of Effect (AoE) poisoning zone.

Hydro: Splendor of Tranquil Waters (Sword)

Ability: Furina Salon Members

[ITEM ICON]
Splendor of Tranquil Waters
[GIF DEMO: Salon Members]
Showcase of the 3 Salon Members spawning and attacking.
  • Passive: Grants permanent Water Breathing.
  • On-Hit: Creates Hydro splash particles.
  • Active (V): Summons 3 Salon Members (Surintendante Chevalmarin, Mademoiselle Crabaletta, Gentilhomme Usher) around the player. They attack enemies but drain the player's HP per hit. If the player drops below 50% HP and stops healing, the members deal no damage. They disappear when the cooldown ends.

Showcase of speed boost and fire elemental blobs roaming.
